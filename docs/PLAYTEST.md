# Playtest notes

Notes from playing on the server, with what the code says about each. Nothing here is built yet; an item moves to
`DECISIONS.md` once it's settled and done.

## 2026-11-02 (first days on the server, 2 players, no enchanted gear, nothing past iron/steel)

| # | Note | What the code says | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Mobs hit very hard; Medieval Armory's rusted sets (`magistuarmory:rusted*`, kastenbrust) are the gear most mobs wear; bosses crush us. | Intended: the pack is meant to be hard this early. | Keep, watch |
| 2 | No crashes. | Since `-Xmx6500M` on the 8 GB plan. | - |
| 3 | Bosses are hard and good. | - | Keep |
| 4 | Iron Stomach misses some bad foods. | It goes by the tag `bloomlevelup:food/unsafe`: vanilla raw meat and fish, rotten flesh, spider eye, pufferfish, poisonous potato, plus the `c`/`forge` raw-meat, raw-fish and food-poisoning tags. A mod's food with a bad effect that isn't in those tags is missed. Possible fix in Bloom: also count any food whose own effects include a harmful one (no list to keep up). The one missed was Born in Chaos's rotten fish (`born_in_chaos_v1:rotten_fish`); Born in Chaos is an MCreator mod, whose foods may give their effects from a script rather than the food's own effects, so the pack should also tag those (optional entries) and a relay check can list the rest. | Fixed (to install) |
| 5 | Timber fells logs that structures placed. | Placed-block memory only knows logs a player placed; worldgen logs count as natural. Timber needs the connected logs to touch natural (non-persistent) leaves, but *any* log in the connected group will do, so a cabin's beams that touch a real tree, or a structure's own tree, pass. Possible fixes: skip logs inside a structure's pieces, or only fell when the cut log stands on a trunk (logs straight down to dirt or grass), which a beam never does. Seen on a house's spruce beam: one log broken, the whole frame went. | Fixed (to install) |
| 6 | Leveling: Armor fastest (40-50), Combat 30-40, Utility and Misc 20-30. Wisdom ~20 and vanilla levels come fast (20-40). | Armor XP comes from damage taken, and mobs hit hard (note 1), so it runs ahead. Wisdom multiplies vanilla XP orbs by 1 + 4% a point (x1.8 at 20), on top of modded mobs' and sentinels' big XP drops. | Not disliked; watch |
| 7 | Companions: owners shouldn't be able to hurt their own. | Each owner hit costs Migamigos tolerance; 0 turns it on you (likely how the Claymore was lost: Better Combat sweeps). Approved. | Fixed (to install) |
| 8 | Reuse Migamigos' hide-armor toggles for players and for Zoni's Companions. | Migamigos has per-piece toggles (`toggleArmorPiece`) on its companion screen. Players would need small toggle buttons in the inventory and a render hook, synced to others; Zoni's would need a hook in its renderer. | Idea, look into |
| 9 | Companion "Cannot Wander" doesn't stop wandering. | Migamigos' own AI: "Cannot Wander" only stops idle strolling while *following*. A *staying* companion runs `MoveBackToPostGoal`, which picks a spot up to 10 blocks out toward its post every few ticks and never checks the toggle, so it mills about. Possible fix in the add-on: while staying with wandering off, only walk back when it's pushed off its post. | Fixed (to install) |
| 10 | Raid's "A new foe has appeared!" came about 3 times, but no Enemigo showed up. Thought: spawned underground. | More likely the add-on: a raid's Enemigo has no owner and no name, so the wild-companion despawn treats it as wild. Raid waves spawn up to ~64 blocks from the village, and the Enemigo isn't a raider (doesn't march in), so past 32 blocks it's gone within a minute or so, past 128 at once. Fix: Enemigos never count as wild companions. | Fixed (to install) |
| 11 | Axes disable shields (players' and mobs'), hammers and other heavy weapons don't. Good balance or misleading? | Vanilla: only `AxeItem`s disable shields; mobs with shields follow the same check. Option: a tag of heavy weapons (Medieval Armory hammers, maces, etc.) that also disable shields, maybe for a shorter time than an axe. | Undecided |
| 12 | Corrupted illagers (The Graveyard) spawn by day in the darker biomes, and where there's no cover they just burn. | The pack's `legionwarex_dark` rule meant "dark", but Easy Mob Spawn Control ignores `maxLight` when a rule also sets block or sky light, so it only asked for open ground without torchlight, at any hour. Now maxLight 7 alone (night, or thick cover by day) and not below height 50. | Fixed (to install) |
